Square V1
Matched to the Market protocol (ripe0x/market, 16 Nov 2026). Every price is in ETH and is the price payable now. A listing is an order holding one NFT, at a fixed price or one that moves to an end price over a duration and then holds; expiry is optional and off by default. An offer is an order holding ETH, for one NFT or several from a collection, with an expiry or none. Listing, cancelling and a first sale to an offer each take two wallet steps. Fee figures use today's 1% rate; each order's fee is read from the protocol.
